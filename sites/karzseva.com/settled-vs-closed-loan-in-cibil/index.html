6. Understanding Settlement's Impact on CIBIL

When a loan is settled, it means the borrower has paid a reduced amount to close the loan, often because they couldn't afford the full repayment. This settlement is recorded on your CIBIL report and significantly impacts your credit score. Unlike a closed loan, which indicates full repayment, a settled loan sends a red flag to lenders about your creditworthiness.

According to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), a settled loan status can reduce your CIBIL score by up to 100 points. This drop can make it challenging to secure future loans or credit cards. Lenders view settlements as a sign of financial distress, making them hesitant to extend credit.
