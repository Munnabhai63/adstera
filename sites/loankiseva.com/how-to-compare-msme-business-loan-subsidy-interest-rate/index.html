8. Case Study: Calculating MSME Business Loan Subsidy Interest

Consider an MSME in Mumbai securing a ₹50 lakh loan from HDFC Bank. The initial interest rate stands at 12%. Government subsidy reduces this by 3%, lowering costs significantly.

Let’s break down the financial impact. Initially, the annual interest expense equals ₹6 lakh. Post-subsidy, it drops to ₹4.5 lakh. This ₹1.5 lakh saving boosts cash flow, aiding reinvestment opportunities.

For a clearer picture, calculate monthly payments using the formula: P = [r*PV] / [1 - (1 + r)^-n]. Here, P is payment, r is monthly interest rate, PV is principal value, n is number of payments.

Without subsidy, monthly payment calculates to approximately ₹52,990. After applying the 3% subsidy, the revised monthly payment reduces to ₹47,321. This difference of ₹5,669 per month can be redirected towards business expansion.

Understanding these calculations empowers MSME owners to make informed decisions. It highlights the tangible benefits of subsidy interest, encouraging prudent financial planning.
