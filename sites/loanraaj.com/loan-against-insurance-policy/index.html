What Is a Loan Against an Insurance Policy?

A loan against an insurance policy is a secured loan in which an eligible life insurance policy is used as security for borrowing. The insurance company or an eligible financial institution provides funds based on the policy’s available surrender value and the applicable loan conditions. The policyholder receives the loan amount and becomes responsible for repaying the principal and interest according to the agreed terms.

The most important concept in this process is surrender value. Surrender value refers to the amount that may become payable if an eligible policyholder voluntarily terminates a policy before its maturity, subject to the policy’s terms and conditions. It is not the same as the sum assured. The sum assured is the insurance benefit specified in the contract, whereas surrender value represents the amount calculated under the insurer’s surrender-value rules.

Which Insurance Policies Are Eligible for a Loan in 2026?

Not every life insurance policy offers a loan facility. Eligibility mainly depends on whether the insurance product has an eligible surrender value and whether its policy document provides borrowing privileges. Some traditional savings-oriented policies may allow borrowing after satisfying minimum premium-payment and policy-duration conditions.

Endowment Insurance Plans

Endowment plans combine life insurance protection with a savings component. Depending on the specific product, these policies may acquire surrender value after the required conditions are fulfilled. Once the policy becomes eligible, the policyholder may be able to borrow against a percentage of the available value. The maximum amount depends on the product terms, policy status, accumulated benefits and outstanding dues.

Money-Back Insurance Plans

Money-back insurance policies provide periodic survival benefits during the policy term along with life insurance protection. Some eligible plans also provide a loan facility. However, the amount available for borrowing can depend on the benefits already paid and the remaining surrender value. Policyholders should request an updated loan quotation from their insurer before applying.

Non-Linked Savings Insurance Plans

Non-linked savings products are an important category under IRDAI’s policy-loan framework. The 2024 Master Circular provides that non-linked savings products offering surrender value shall have a policy loan facility based on the eligible surrender value. The actual loan percentage, interest rate and repayment conditions are still governed by the individual product provisions.

Certain Annuity Products

IRDAI’s 2024 Master Circular also allows insurers to offer policy loans under certain annuity products with a Return of Purchase Price option. This is subject to the product’s conditions and should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every annuity policy provides a loan.

Can You Get a Loan Against a Term Insurance Policy?

A pure term insurance policy generally does not provide a conventional loan facility because it normally does not accumulate surrender value. Term insurance is mainly designed to provide financial protection to the family if the insured person dies during the coverage period. Some special products may have different conditions, so policyholders should check their actual contract.

Eligibility Criteria for Loan Against Insurance Policy 2026

Loan Agains Before applying, policyholders should understand the basic eligibility requirements. These requirements can vary depending on whether the loan is being offered directly by the insurance company or by a bank that accepts an insurance policy as security.

First, the applicant must own an eligible insurance policy that provides a loan facility or is accepted by the lending institution. The policy must satisfy the applicable conditions, and the policyholder should be legally entitled to offer it as security. A policy with insufficient surrender value may not qualify for borrowing, even if it has a large sum assured.

Second, the policy must have acquired the required surrender value. Different insurance products have different premium-payment and policy-duration conditions. Some policies may permit loans after the first full year’s premium, while other products may have different requirements. Therefore, there is no single minimum policy age applicable to every insurance product.

How Much Loan Can You Get Against an Insurance Policy?

The loan amount is generally determined using the eligible surrender value and the percentage permitted under the policy conditions. It is not automatically calculated from the total sum assured.

For example, consider a policyholder who has an eligible surrender value of ₹5 lakh. If the insurer allows borrowing up to 80% of that value, the potential loan would be ₹4 lakh. If the permitted percentage is 70%, the potential amount would be ₹3.5 lakh.

LIC’s published policy conditions state that the maximum loan available under the relevant provisions can be up to 90% of surrender value, including the cash value of bonus, and up to 85% for paid-up policies. These limits should not be treated as universal for every LIC product. The individual policy bond determines the actual loan facility.

Interest Rate on Loan Against Insurance Policy 2026

Interest rate is one of the most important factors to consider before borrowing. There is no single interest rate applicable to every insurance policy loan in India. The rate depends on the insurance company, product conditions and, where applicable, the lending institution.

Some insurers declare policy-specific interest rates periodically. A bank providing a loan against an insurance policy may have a separate interest structure, processing charges and repayment conditions. The borrower should therefore obtain a written quotation before accepting the loan.

For example, LIC states that its policy-loan interest rate is declared by the Corporation and is plan-specific. Its published policy conditions also explain that interest is payable half-yearly under the applicable provisions. The exact rate should be confirmed for the policy and loan period concerned.

Documents Required for Loan Against Insurance Policy

The document requirements can differ between insurance companies and lending institutions. However, applicants are commonly asked to provide their original policy document, completed loan application form, PAN card, Aadhaar card or another accepted identity document, address proof, bank account details and a cancelled cheque or bank verification document.

The insurer may also request a recent photograph, premium-payment details, policy number, assignment form and signed loan agreement. If the application is being made through a bank rather than directly through the insurer, the bank may ask for additional information, such as income proof or other financial documents.

How to Apply for a Loan Against Insurance Policy in 2026

The application process is generally straightforward when the policy is eligible and the applicant has the necessary documents. However, the exact procedure can differ depending on whether the loan is provided directly by the insurer or through a bank.

Step 1: Check Your Insurance Policy

Loan Agains Start by reading your policy document carefully. Look for sections such as Policy Loan, Loan Facility, Conditions and Privileges or Surrender Value. These sections explain whether the policy provides borrowing privileges and what conditions must be satisfied.

If the information is unclear, contact the insurer’s official customer-care service or visit the servicing branch. Do not rely on an agent’s verbal assurance without checking the actual policy terms.

Step 2: Request Your Latest Surrender Value

Contact the insurer and request the current eligible surrender value of your policy. This information is important because the loan amount is generally linked to this value rather than the total sum assured. Also ask whether any previous loan or unpaid interest is recorded against the policy.

Step 3: Check Your Maximum Loan Eligibility

Ask the insurer or lender to calculate the maximum amount available. Confirm the permitted percentage of surrender value, applicable interest rate, processing fees and repayment conditions. Request the calculation in writing so that you can understand how the amount has been determined.

Step 4: Complete the Application Form

Fill out the policy loan application form with your personal details, policy number, contact information, bank account details and requested loan amount. Check that the information matches your KYC and insurance records to avoid unnecessary verification problems.

Step 5: Submit the Required Documents

Submit the application form and supporting documents through the insurer’s authorised branch or online channel. If the original policy document is required, obtain an acknowledgement or receipt. Keep copies of all documents for your own records.

Step 6: Complete Assignment Formalities

The insurer or lender may require the policy to be assigned as security for the loan. Assignment gives the lender specified rights under the insurance contract until the loan and interest are settled. Read the assignment form and loan agreement carefully before signing.

Step 7: Verification and Loan Approval

The institution checks the policy details, surrender value, applicant information, outstanding dues and supporting documents. If everything meets the requirements, the loan may be approved. If additional information is required, the institution may ask the applicant to provide clarification.

Step 8: Receive the Loan Amount

After approval and completion of the required formalities, the loan amount is disbursed according to the institution’s process. It is generally transferred to the verified bank account. Keep the sanction letter, loan account information, interest rate and repayment instructions safely.

How to Apply for an LIC Policy Loan?

LIC policyholders can check whether their policy provides a loan facility by reviewing the conditions printed in their policy bond. LIC also provides an online policy loan request facility for registered Premier Services customers.

A typical process involves visiting the official LIC website, logging in to the customer portal, checking the policy’s loan eligibility and registering a loan request if the facility is available. After registering the request, the required loan documents can be submitted to a nearby LIC branch according to the instructions provided by the Corporation.

Repayment Options for an Insurance Policy Loan

Repayment conditions depend on the insurer and the particular loan arrangement. Some lenders require repayment of the principal and interest according to a fixed schedule, while certain policy loans allow the borrower to pay interest periodically and settle the principal later.

Under regular repayment, the borrower pays the outstanding amount according to the agreed schedule. This can help reduce the loan balance and control the total interest cost. Some arrangements also permit partial repayment, allowing the borrower to reduce the outstanding principal before the final settlement.

Benefits of Taking a Loan Against an Insurance Policy

One of the main benefits of a policy loan is that it may provide access to funds without requiring the policyholder to immediately surrender an eligible insurance contract. This can be useful when someone needs money for medical expenses, education fees, business requirements or an unexpected family expense.

Loan Against Insurance Policy vs Surrendering the Policy

Some policyholders consider surrendering their insurance policy when they need money urgently. However, surrendering a policy and borrowing against it are two different financial decisions.

When an eligible policy is surrendered, the insurer pays the applicable surrender value and the insurance contract generally terminates. Future insurance cover and benefits under that contract cease. When a policy loan is taken, the contract may continue according to its terms, but the borrower remains responsible for the principal and interest.

For example, suppose your policy has an eligible surrender value of ₹3 lakh. You may be able to borrow a permitted percentage of this amount while retaining the policy. If you surrender the policy, you receive the applicable surrender value and give up the future benefits under the contract.

Common Mistakes to Avoid Before Applying

One common mistake is assuming that every insurance policy provides a loan. Pure term plans generally do not offer conventional policy loans because they normally do not build surrender value. ULIPs are also subject to separate rules and should not be treated as eligible for a normal policy loan.

Another mistake is focusing only on the maximum loan amount without checking the interest rate. Borrowers should understand the total cost of borrowing, including interest calculation, payment frequency and charges. Taking the maximum amount without a repayment plan can create financial pressure later.
