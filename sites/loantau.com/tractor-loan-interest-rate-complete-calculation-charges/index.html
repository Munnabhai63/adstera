9. Common Mistakes in Tractor Loan Interest Rate Calculation

Calculating tractor loan interest rates can be complex, and borrowers often make several mistakes:

  • Ignoring Processing Fees: Many borrowers overlook the processing fees charged by banks, which can add 1-2% to the total loan cost.
  • Assuming Simple Interest: Tractor loans typically use compound interest, so assuming simple interest can lead to underestimation of total repayments.
  • Not Comparing Rates: Different financial institutions offer varying interest rates and terms. Failing to compare these can result in higher costs.
  • Overlooking Prepayment Penalties: Some lenders charge penalties for early repayment, which can impact the overall cost if the loan is repaid before the term ends.
  • Misunderstanding Loan Tenure Impact: Longer loan tenures reduce monthly installments but increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.
