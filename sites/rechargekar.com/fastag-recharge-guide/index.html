FASTag Online Recharge Guide 2026: Instant UPI, Banks & App Top-Up

FASTag is an electronic toll collection system operated by the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) using Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology to enable seamless toll deductions without vehicle stoppage. Keeping an active FASTag balance avoids statutory double cash toll penalties at national and state highway plazas across India.

Step-by-Step UPI FASTag Recharge Protocol

To top up any FASTag instantly via UPI, enter the standardized Virtual Payment Address: netc.VehicleRegistrationNumber@bankhandle (e.g., netc.DL01AB1234@sbi for SBI FASTag or netc.MH02CD5678@icici for ICICI FASTag). Enter the desired recharge amount and authorize the payment via your secure UPI PIN.

NHAI One Vehicle One FASTag Compliance Guidelines

Under NHAI's One Vehicle, One FASTag initiative, vehicle owners must ensure complete KYC compliance with their tag-issuing bank. Linking multiple tags to a single vehicle or holding incomplete KYC tags results in tag blacklisting by the NPCI central toll clearing house, leading to toll plaza gate barriers failing to lift automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I enter a FASTag lane with low balance?

If your FASTag balance falls below the minimum threshold, toll plaza scanners will mark the tag as blacklisted, requiring cash payment at double the standard toll rate.

How can I check my current FASTag balance instantly?

You can check your balance via the 'My FASTag' mobile app, your issuing bank's portal, or by sending an SMS to the toll balance enquiry number 5676766.

Can a FASTag be transferred to another vehicle?

No, FASTag RFID chips are strictly mapped to the specific vehicle registration number and chassis number and cannot be transferred to a different vehicle.
