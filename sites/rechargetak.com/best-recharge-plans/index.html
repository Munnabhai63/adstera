Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when my daily prepaid data limit is reached?

Once your daily high-speed quota is exhausted, internet browsing speed is throttled to 64 Kbps for the remainder of the calendar day until resetting at midnight.

Can I activate a data booster pack on an active plan?

Yes, all major telecom operators offer add-on data booster packs starting from ₹19 for 1GB up to ₹65 for 6GB that function seamlessly over your existing validity.

How do I port my mobile number to another operator without changing numbers?

Send an SMS containing 'PORT <10-digit mobile number>' to 1900 to receive your Unique Porting Code (UPC), then visit any store of your chosen new telecom operator.
