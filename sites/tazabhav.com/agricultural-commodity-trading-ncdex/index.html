  • Mistake 2 — Ignoring Inter-State Mandi Cess & Transit Tolls: Hidden costs like inter-state mandi cess and transit tolls can erode profit margins. These charges vary across states, complicating budget forecasts. Traders must account for these expenses when calculating procurement costs.   • Mistake 4 — Inadequate Warehousing & Fumigation Scheduling: Storing grains in uncertified facilities invites insect infestations and fungal growth. Inadequate fumigation schedules exacerbate spoilage issues, resulting in substantial losses. Certified warehouses with regular pest control measures are essential for preserving crop quality. Neglecting proper storage protocols undermines long-term profitability and sustainability.
  • Mistake 5 — Misjudging Arrival Timing During Peak Glut: Panic buying before peak arrivals can lead to overpayment. Seasonal gluts drive prices down, hitting seasonal lows. Traders must accurately predict arrival timings to capitalize on favorable market conditions. Misjudging these dynamics results in suboptimal purchases, eroding profit margins and hindering effective risk management.
