Privacy Policy & Consumer Data Protection Standard

Last Updated: November 2026 Version 4.2 Applicable Law: DPDP Act (India), GDPR & CCPA

Welcome to TazaBhav (accessible at https://tazabhav.com). We take consumer transparency, data privacy, and digital trust with the utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal and non-personal data collected, recorded, and processed when you interact with our editorial content, financial comparisons, and digital calculators.

1. Institutional Identity & Ownership

TazaBhav operates as an independent digital publishing organization and consumer research resource. Our primary operational objective is to provide objective comparisons, regulatory guides, and mathematical analysis across consumer finance, telecom, utility, and commercial sectors. We do not operate as a direct banking institution, non-banking financial company (NBFC), insurance underwriter, or telecom operator.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. Depending on your engagement on our platform, we may collect:

  • Interactive Calculator Input Data: Values entered into our on-site calculation widgets (such as loan amounts, repayment tenures, interest percentages, or recharge figures) are processed locally within your web browser client. We do not transmit or store your raw calculation inputs on our remote servers.
  • Technical Log Files: Like most standard web servers, our edge infrastructure automatically logs access events. This includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is non-personally identifiable and is utilized strictly for system administration, fraud detection, and site optimization.
  • Voluntary Inquiries: If you contact our editorial desk via email or web form, we collect your provided name, email address, and message contents solely to address your grievance or editorial inquiry.

3. Cookies, Web Beacons & Advertising Partners

TazaBhav uses standard cookies to store visitor preferences and optimize user experience. Our third-party advertising network partners (including Google AdSense, Adsterra, and Monetag) may utilize cookies and web beacons on our site to serve relevant contextual advertisements based on your prior browsing history.

You maintain the fundamental right to disable cookies through your personal web browser configuration settings. Detailed cookie management instructions are accessible within your specific browser's help documentation.

4. Third-Party Links & External Portals

Our editorial guides may contain hyperlinks to official governmental portals, regulatory agencies (such as RBI, TRAI, IRDAI, NPCI), commercial banks, or verified corporate websites. Once you click an external link and depart TazaBhav, you are governed by the third party's independent privacy protocols. We strongly advise reviewing the privacy terms of any third-party portal you visit.

5. Data Retention & Security Architecture

We deploy robust enterprise-grade SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption across our entire edge delivery network. We never sell, rent, commercialize, or lease consumer personal records to external third-party telemarketers or unauthorized data brokers under any circumstances.

6. Your Rights Under Global Data Frameworks

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain:

  • The Right to Access your stored personal records.
  • The Right to Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • The Right to Restrict or Object to automated data processing.

7. Grievance Redressal & Editorial Contact

In accordance with statutory Information Technology regulations, any inquiries or grievances regarding our privacy architecture should be directed to our appointed Data Protection Officer via our Contact Portal.
