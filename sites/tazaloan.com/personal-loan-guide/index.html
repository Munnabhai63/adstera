Credit Score Requirements & Fast-Track Digital Underwriting

Maintaining a CIBIL score of 750 or higher qualifies borrowers for accelerated paperless loan approvals and lower interest margins. Applicants must submit verified government identification (PAN, Aadhaar), recent address proof, and 3 to 6 months of bank account transaction statements via account aggregators.

Reserve Bank of India Consumer Protections & Transparent KFS

RBI directives require all regulated lenders to deliver a comprehensive Key Fact Statement (KFS) prior to loan disbursement, specifying processing costs, late payment charges, and the annualized percentage rate. Zero foreclosure penalties apply on individual floating-rate personal loans across all scheduled banks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum income to qualify for a personal loan?

Most commercial banks require salaried applicants to earn a minimum net monthly salary of ₹25,000, while fintech lenders consider monthly incomes from ₹15,000.

Can personal loans be used for debt consolidation?

Yes, consolidating multiple high-interest credit card debts into a single lower-rate personal loan reduces monthly outflow and accelerates debt freedom.

How is the monthly repayment EMI calculated?

Personal loan EMI is computed using standard amortizing formulas balancing principal reduction against declining monthly interest charges.
